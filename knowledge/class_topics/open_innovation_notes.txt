OPEN INNOVATION — CLASS TOPIC

What are Corporates Doing?

The class material compares examples involving P&G, LEGO, ISRO and PHILIPS.

The examples examine:
- Source of idea / innovation
- Whether the source is external or internal
- Direction of flow of knowledge or innovation
- Main actor initiating the move
- Purpose of the main actor
- Value for the corporate / main actor
- Value for the partner
- Ownership / control of knowledge

Examples discussed in the class material include:

P&G:
Source of idea / innovation: Outsiders / External
Direction of flow: Outside to Inside
Main actor initiating the move: Outsiders
Purpose mentioned in the class material includes increasing market reach.

LEGO:
Source of idea / innovation: Outsiders / External
Direction of flow: Outside to Inside
Main actor initiating the move: Outsiders
Purposes mentioned include revenue / profit and becoming more innovative.
Value mentioned includes validated ideas.

ISRO:
Source of idea / innovation: Internal / Inside
Direction of flow: Inside to outside
Purposes mentioned include reducing import dependence, private-sector participation, ecosystem building and generating revenue.

PHILIPS:
Source of idea / innovation: Internal / Inside
Direction of flow: Inside to outside
Purpose includes a marketing move, saving R&D time and cost, and validated ideas.
The material also refers to ownership / control of knowledge.

Value for partners discussed in the material includes:
- Brand recognition
- Global market access
- Revenues
- Saving R&D cost and time
- Access to advanced technology
- Access to customers

Knowledge Landscape:
The class material asks students to find examples for the next class.
