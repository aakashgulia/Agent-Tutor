Verified Panasonic Startup Collaboration Examples

1. Co.lab Studio by IGNITION

Panasonic launched Co.lab Studio by IGNITION in February 2026 to co-create scalable digital services with startups. The programme is described by Panasonic as a business-first incubation and integration platform anchored to MirAIe.

Panasonic partnered with LINK Innovation as the operating partner for the programme.

The programme focuses on pilots, integrations and developing scalable services with startups.

Source:
Official Panasonic India News, 17 February 2026
"Panasonic launches ‘Co.lab Studio by IGNITION’ to co-create scalable digital services with startups"

Verification status:
Verified through official Panasonic source.


2. Portl and Sensio

In June 2026, Panasonic announced Portl and Sensio as its first co-business creation partners under its startup collaboration efforts.

The collaboration is described as involving the co-development of AI-powered wellness and wearable solutions for MirAIe.

Panasonic stated that the first cycle received 173 applications from more than 20 countries.

Source:
Official Panasonic India News, 30 June 2026
"Panasonic redefines enterprise-startup collaboration, backing India-built AI startups to power the next chapter of connected living"

Verification status:
Verified through official Panasonic source.


3. Red Health and Respirer Living Sciences

In December 2025, Panasonic announced Red Health and Respirer Living Sciences as joint winners of Ignition 3.0.

Panasonic stated that the two startups would partner with Panasonic to co-create scalable solutions.

The same announcement also identified Ambiator, Biomoneta, Cantata and Kubo Care as ACCELERATE winners that would work on pilots and explore strategic partnerships.

Source:
Official Panasonic India News, 22 December 2025
"Panasonic deepens its bet on India’s Residential Tech Ecosystem with the next evolution of Ignition startups – Red Health & Respirer Living Sciences"

Verification status:
Verified through official Panasonic source.

In April 2026, Panasonic announced a Co.lab Studio by IGNITION Roadshow in Bangalore.

Panasonic stated that it had partnered with LINK Innovation as the operating partner.

The announcement stated that the programme focuses on pilots, deployment and integration.
